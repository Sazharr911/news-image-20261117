   Trump and Xi at APEC (feeds).
4. **AFRICA · HEALTH** — Ebola cases in DR Congo top 11,000, WHO says.
   Cases passed 11,000 with 5,400 deaths, more than doubled in three
   months (UN News via One Turn and digests).
5. **GLOBAL · CLIMATE** — COP31 divided over a fossil-fuel phase-out.
   Australia, co-host, tabled an 18-page first draft in Antalya on
   16 Nov; competing "transitioning away" vs "phase out" wording;
   adaptation finance tripling to $120bn by 2035 proposed; talks close
   Friday (Reuters).

   status. The US lost elimination status after 26 years amid record
   case numbers (BBC-style feeds). US 2026 cases reported as 5,412 in
   one same-day brief and 5,700+ in others, so the page says "5,400+",
   which both reports support.
7. **AMERICAS · BUSINESS** — Nvidia's quarterly sales double to
   $115.8bn. Q3 revenue $115.8bn, about double a year earlier, ~$130bn
   guide; the board will not reinstate ousted CEO Jensen Huang;
   earlier the Dow fell 849 points (-1.6%) (feeds, One Turn).
8. **MIDDLE EAST · GAZA** — Gaza truce at risk of collapse, UN is
   told. UN agencies say 1.4m face acute hunger; the UN migration
   agency warns of hypothermia deaths without more winter supplies
   (Reuters brief); UK, Australia, Canada and Italy urge Israel to
   open crossings (Asas Press).
9. **ASIA · POLITICS** — Mongolia's president says he will resign.
   Khurelsukh denies a $25m claim by ex-mayor Nyambaatar linking him
   to the Tavantolgoi-Gashuunsukhait railway contract; Bodi
   International denies paying bribes; the State Great Khural must
   accept the resignation (AP, Pollar, RIX).
10. **EUROPE · POLITICS** — Serbia's student-led majority takes its
    seats. The Student List won 140 of 250 seats in the 25 October
    election; parliament convened 17 November, ending 14 years of
    Vučić's party rule (103 seats, now opposition) (Pollar).
11. **ASIA · LABOUR** — Bangladesh police tear-gas garment workers.
    Strike over the minimum wage: workers demand Tk 30,000 a month, up
    from 12,500; factories shut across Savar, Ashulia and Gazipur
    (Asas Press; closure counts differ between reports — ~37 in some,
    ~150 sites in Asas — so the page says "dozens of factories shut").
12. **ASIA · AIR CRASH** — Russian An-12 crash in Siberia kills all
    19. Air Force transport came down near Yagunovo, Kemerovo region;
    the defence ministry says a technical fault is suspected (Reuters
    via feeds, ASX News).
13. **AFRICA · JUSTICE** — Mozambique puts opposition leader on
    trial. Venâncio Mondlane faces 5 charges at the Supreme Court in
    Maputo over the deadly 2024 post-election protests; he denies
    wrongdoing (Asas Press).
14. **GLOBAL · SPACE** — Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth.
    First spacecraft to do so (~173 AU out); its signals now take a
    full day; NASA invites people to shine lights after sunset
    (Pollar science desk; milestone due 18 Nov, reported Tuesday).
